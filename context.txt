Wavelength Music — Product & Support FAQ

Subscription Plans:
Wavelength offers four tiers: Free (ad-supported, shuffle-only on mobile), Student
($4.99/month, requires yearly re-verification), Premium ($9.99/month, ad-free with
unlimited skips), and Family ($14.99/month, up to 6 linked accounts with individual
listening profiles). Annual billing on Premium or Family gets a 15% discount over
monthly billing.

Cancellation Policy:
Subscribers can cancel anytime from Account Settings. Cancellation takes effect at the
end of the current billing cycle, with no prorated refund for unused days. Once
cancelled, the account reverts to Free at the next renewal date, and offline downloads
plus uploaded personal tracks stop syncing immediately, though playlists and library
history are preserved for 12 months in case of resubscription.

Audio Quality:
Free-tier streams are capped at 128kbps AAC. Premium and Student streams go up to
320kbps AAC. Family plan members each get up to 320kbps independently on their own
linked account. A Hi-Fi add-on ($4.99/month, requires Premium or Family) unlocks
lossless FLAC streaming up to 16-bit/44.1kHz.

Account Sharing:
Family plan accounts support up to 6 separate logins, each requiring the same
registered household address, re-verified twice yearly via app location or IP check.
Premium and Student accounts are single-user only; sharing login credentials violates
the Terms of Service and may result in suspension. Each linked Family member keeps a
private listening history unless they opt in to the shared "Family Mix" playlist.

Offline Download Policy:
Premium, Family, and Student subscribers can download up to 10,000 songs for offline
listening, synced across a maximum of 5 devices. Downloaded tracks require the app to
verify the subscription online at least once every 30 days, or offline playback is
disabled until the next successful sync. Free-tier users cannot download tracks.

Upload Policy:
Premium and Family subscribers may upload their own personal music files to blend with
their library, up to 25,000 tracks per account. Supported formats are MP3, M4A, and
FLAC, with a 250MB limit per file. Uploaded tracks sync across devices like any other
library item but do not count against the offline download limit.
